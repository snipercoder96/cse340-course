HASHING vs Encryption


Encryption

🔒 Secret Recipe Analogy  
Imagine you have a family recipe written in plain language. To protect it, you rewrite the recipe using a secret code that only you and trusted family members understand. To anyone else, the recipe looks like nonsense — scrambled words and symbols. But with the secret codebook (the key), you can always translate it back into the original recipe, perfectly intact.

It is reversible.

Hashing

🍞 Bread Analogy
Imagine baking bread:

You start with flour, water, yeast (the data).

Once baked, you get bread (the hash).

You can check the bread to confirm it came from those ingredients, but you can’t turn the bread back into flour and water.

That’s why hashing is one-way — irreversible.
